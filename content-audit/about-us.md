About Us

Systemizer Technic is one of the leading enterprise solutions companies in Malaysia. Powered by a team of qualified and experienced IT professionals, coupled with over a decade of industry experience, Systemizer Technic has successfully supported its clients nationwide.

The Company

Incorporated in 2008 as the YTY Group Industries’ IT arm saw us managing several mission-critical servers and applications. In 2010, we moved to become independent, to grow and evolve into a prominent solution integrator for Infrastructure System Solutions. In 2016, Systemizer Technic became a subsidiary of the Setia Ibarat Group, with a widened portfolio of offerings designed to support the ever-growing need for reliable IT support services.

With an extensive portfolio of technology solutions under one roof, we help our clients to modernize technology, reimagine processes and transform experiences so they can stay ahead in our fast-changing world. Our expertise is driven by out-of-the-box thinking and technical knowledge, enabling the team to be ready to deliver a full range of data center solutions designed to meet our clients’ digital needs.
