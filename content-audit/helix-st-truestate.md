ST TrueState

Stay True to Your IT State

A configuration drift tracking and management service that identifies unexpected changes, records every deviation, and brings systems back to the intended baseline to keep everything aligned.

Overview

ST TrueState is an agentless configuration assurance platform designed to actively manage the state of your infrastructure. Powered by the Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform (RHAAP), it detects configuration drift and gives IT teams the control to decide whether to align systems back to the “gold standard” or update the baseline to reflect authorized changes.

Key Features

Unified Infrastructure Visibility

Gain a single dashboard that monitors servers, VMs, storage, databases, networks, and public clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP) in real-time.

Automated Remediation

Reduce manual troubleshooting by configuring automatic responses to common alerts, such as service restarts or executing custom workflows.

Highly Customisable Logic

Build tailored checks and specific monitoring rules that fit unique operational requirements rather than relying on generic thresholds.

Broad Platform Support

Seamlessly monitors diverse environments including Linux, Windows, VMware, Proxmox, Docker, and Kubernetes.

Key Features

Drift Management

Detects deviations from your defined baseline and provides the flexibility to either revert the drift or commit the change as the new configuration standard.

Agentless Architecture

Utilizes the Red Hat Ansible engine to execute checks and fixes remotely, eliminating the performance impact and management burden of installing agents.

Full Change Visibility

Tracks every configuration change: what, when, and where. It provides a clear audit trail for compliance, governance, and troubleshooting.

Flexible Remediation

Can be set to automatically revert unauthorized changes for security or be used to validate and merge drifts that improve the environment.

The Strategic Advantage

TrueState turns configuration drift from a hidden risk into a managed process. It acknowledges that systems evolve, providing the visibility to distinguish between accidental “drift” and intentional improvements. This allows IT teams to maintain compliance without stalling innovation, instantly reverting unauthorized changes while easily adopting new configurations when necessary.
