STackNode

Virtualisation That Makes Sense

A value-focused and locally supported infrastructure stack that delivers full capability without unnecessary cost. Comes with zero-cost migration services and enterprise-grade stability.

Overview

STackNode is an enterprise-ready, on-premise infrastructure solution that provides a powerful alternative to proprietary virtualization. By combining Proxmox Virtualisation with validated hardware and professional support, it delivers a future-proof platform that escapes expensive licensing cycles.

STackNode GUI

Key Features

Unified Infrastructure Visibility

Gain a single dashboard that monitors servers, VMs, storage, databases, networks, and public clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP) in real-time.

Automated Remediation

Reduce manual troubleshooting by configuring automatic responses to common alerts, such as service restarts or executing custom workflows.

Highly Customisable Logic

Build tailored checks and specific monitoring rules that fit unique operational requirements rather than relying on generic thresholds.

Broad Platform Support

Seamlessly monitors diverse environments including Linux, Windows, VMware, Proxmox, Docker, and Kubernetes.

Key Features

70% Licensing Savings

Significantly reduce IT overhead by moving workloads to an open-source, high-performance hypervisor.

Flexible Architecture

Deploy as Hyper-Converged Infrastructure (HCI) with software-defined storage (Ceph) or a traditional 3-tier setup.

Hardware Freedom

Use certified new hardware or repurpose existing servers to maximize ROI and extend asset lifespan.

Seamless Transition

Includes complimentary infrastructure assessments and zero-cost migration services (P2V/V2V) to move from legacy platforms like VMware.

The Strategic Advantage

STackNode allows organizations to escape expensive licensing cycles without sacrificing performance or enterprise stability. It provides a cost-effective path to modernization while extending the lifespan of existing hardware assets through flexible deployment options.
