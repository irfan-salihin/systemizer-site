Your business needs a backup and disaster recovery plan to protect your data and ensure that all of your critical files and applications are backed up regularly and securely. When it comes to disaster recovery, you want peace of mind that your data is safe and recoverable at all times. The solution is managed IT Backup & Disaster recovery.

When a disaster strikes, your systems may be down for an extended period, which can negatively impact your business’ operations. A fully managed backup and disaster recovery as a service plan ensures that your systems are up and running as quickly as possible, reducing downtime and ensuring business continuity.

Outsourcing your backup & disaster recovery is cost-effective solution compared to traditional backup and disaster recovery methods because it’s all-in-one—it includes everything you need for protection against data loss or corruption. And if something goes wrong with your system, the service provider will help you recover any data that is lost or corrupted and access to aplications will be restored.

Managed backup and recovery as a service (BRaaS) can provide several benefits to an organisation, including:

- Data Protection : Managed BRaaS can ensure that all critical data is regularly backed up, encrypted and stored securely, providing a high level of protection against data loss or corruption due to hardware failure, natural disasters, cyber-attacks, or human error.

- Cost Savings : Managed BRaaS eliminates the need for an organisation to invest in expensive backup infrastructure and IT staff, as the service provider manages the entire backup and recovery process, reducing overall costs.

- Scalability : Managed BRaaS can easily scale to accommodate an organisation’s changing backup and recovery needs, as it eliminates the need for additional hardware and IT staff, providing greater flexibility and agility.

- Automated and Efficient : Managed BRaaS automates the entire backup and recovery process, reducing the time and effort required by IT staff to manage backups and restore data, allowing them to focus on other critical tasks.

- Increased Reliability: Managed BRaaS service providers often offer a high level of reliability, with guaranteed uptime and fast recovery times, ensuring that critical data is always available when needed.

- Compliance : Managed BRaaS can help organisations comply with data protection regulations, by providing secure backup and recovery processes that meet regulatory requirements.

Managed backup and disaster recovery as a service can provide an organisation with a cost-effective, scalable, and reliable solution for backing up and recovering critical data, while also ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.
