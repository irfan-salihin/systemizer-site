## Overview

ST Overwatch is a highly customisable IT monitoring and automation platform designed to provide real-time visibility into your entire infrastructure. Unlike rigid, traditional monitoring tools, it adapts to your specific environment, using lightweight agents to collect deep telemetry from servers, storage systems, cloud infrastructure and network devices.

## Key Features

**Unified Infrastructure Visibility** — Gain a single dashboard that monitors servers, VMs, storage, databases, networks, and public clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP) in real-time.

**Automated Remediation** — Reduce manual troubleshooting by configuring automatic responses to common alerts, such as service restarts or executing custom workflows.

**Highly Customisable Logic** — Build tailored checks and specific monitoring rules that fit unique operational requirements rather than relying on generic thresholds.

**Broad Platform Support** — Seamlessly monitors diverse environments including Linux, Windows, VMware, Proxmox, Docker, and Kubernetes.

## The Strategic Advantage

This platform shifts IT operations from reactive to proactive monitoring. By offering deep customization and automated responses, it reduces the manual burden on IT teams and ensures systems remain reliable without the "one-size-fits-all" limitations of legacy monitoring tools.
