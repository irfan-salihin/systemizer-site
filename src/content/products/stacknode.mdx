## Overview

STackNode is an enterprise-ready, on-premise infrastructure solution that provides a powerful alternative to proprietary virtualization. By combining Proxmox Virtualisation with validated hardware and professional support, it delivers a future-proof platform that escapes expensive licensing cycles.

## Key Features

**70% Licensing Savings** — Significantly reduce IT overhead by moving workloads to an open-source, high-performance hypervisor.

**Flexible Architecture** — Deploy as Hyper-Converged Infrastructure (HCI) with software-defined storage (Ceph) or a traditional 3-tier setup.

**Hardware Freedom** — Use certified new hardware or repurpose existing servers to maximize ROI and extend asset lifespan.

**Seamless Transition** — Includes complimentary infrastructure assessments and zero-cost migration services (P2V/V2V) to move from legacy platforms like VMware.
