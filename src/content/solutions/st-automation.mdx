> The average hourly cost of an infrastructure failure is $100,000. Most misconfigurations are introduced through inadvertent human error. — **IDC**

Automation runs repetitive processes with minimal human involvement — from single tasks to self-sufficient workflows that respond to events. Done right, it makes operations faster, more consistent, and more secure.

## Our approach

- **Discovery** — Understand your environment and goals, and find where automation delivers most value.
- **Implementation** — Design, build, and deploy automation, with documentation and collaboration throughout.
- **Support** — Around-the-clock technical support to keep everything reliable.
- **Training** — Enable your team to use, understand, and extend the automation.

## Use cases

- **DR Automation** — Automate failover and recovery testing to cut downtime.
- **Patching & Updates** — Keep servers secure and consistent across the estate.
- **Configuration Drift Detection** — Detect and correct changes automatically.
- **User & Access Management** — Provision and revoke accounts and permissions without delays.
- **Cloud Resource Scheduling** — Start and stop resources by time or workload to lower cloud spend.
- **Security & Compliance Audits** — Scheduled configuration and baseline checks, with clear reports.
- **Infrastructure Provisioning** — Deliver servers, containers, and networks from templates.

## Benefits

- **Fewer errors, more consistency** — Repeatable operations instead of manual guesswork.
- **Faster delivery** — Hours or days of work completed in minutes.
- **Stronger security & compliance** — Automated patching, audits, and access control.
- **Lower operational cost** — Less rework and less dependency on manual effort.
- **Better use of your team** — Engineers freed for higher-value work.

## The case for DR automation

Manual disaster recovery is slow, error-prone, and hard to prove. Automating it makes recovery minutes instead of days, and provides the audit trails required by frameworks such as **RMiT**.

- **Time savings** — Vital systems restored quickly, with less costly downtime.
- **Measurable analytics** — Track performance and improve recovery from real test data.
- **Reduced compliance risk** — Detailed records without post-event reconstruction.
- **Heightened productivity** — Automated failover keeps revenue-generating systems online.
