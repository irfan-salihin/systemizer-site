> Cloud done right means speed, flexibility, and lower costs — without giving up control of your infrastructure.

We help you move, modernise, and manage cloud infrastructure with full visibility. Whether you're shifting off legacy, extending to hybrid, or managing multi-cloud complexity, we tailor the journey and run the day-to-day so your team can focus on outcomes.

## What we deliver

- **Continuity & Resilience** — Cloud failover, automated backups, hybrid DR, and cloud-to-on-prem rollback.
- **Adoption & Migration** — Readiness and gap assessments, application migration, public cloud onboarding, and legacy re-architecture.
- **Architecture & Enablement** — Private cloud, hybrid integration, multi-cloud, and on-demand dev/test/production environments.
- **Operations & Management** — A unified management platform with usage, cost, governance, security, and CI/CD.

## Where it makes sense

- Replacing legacy DR with cloud-based DR.
- Moving from physical servers to public or private cloud.
- Extending on-prem workloads to hybrid cloud.
- Standing up cloud environments for development and testing.
- Managing multiple clouds under a single control panel.
- Controlling cost and preventing overprovisioning.

## Use cases

- **Cloud Bursting** — When private capacity is reached, excess workloads burst to public cloud, preventing interruptions during demand surges and reducing capital spend.
- **Cloud DR** — Keep systems on-premise and back up to the cloud, then fail over with minimal disruption and without the cost of a physical DR centre.
- **App Development & Testing** — Spin up dev/test environments on demand for cost savings, flexibility, and faster time to market.
